# Code of Conduct

This project follows the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.
It applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews) and wherever someone represents the project.

## In short

We want this to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
whatever their experience, background or identity. In practice:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback graciously.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, trolling,
  personal or political attacks, or publishing others' private information.
- Help newcomers: everyone was new once.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to
the maintainers listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). To reach them
privately, use this repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/sangmeshcp/bigmuddy-network-telemetry-pipeline/security/advisories/new)
and start the title with **"Code of Conduct"**. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of
the reporter.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
In order of severity: a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a
permanent ban from project spaces. A maintainer who is the subject of a
report takes no part in handling it.
